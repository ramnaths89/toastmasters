Guide

1 · Start here — Quick

Three buttons that answer the usual questions straight away.

Today shows only clubs meeting today. This week covers the next seven days. Both also move the calendar to this month.

Starred narrows everything to the clubs you have starred. It stays greyed out until you star your first one — see card 5.

2 · The filter buttons

Each row filters by one thing — Division, Language, Week, Day, Time, Format. Tap a button to switch it on, tap it again to switch it off.

Buttons in the same row add up: Tue + Thu means either day. Buttons in different rows narrow: Division Y + Thu means Division Y clubs that meet on a Thursday.

The small number is how many clubs you'd have if you added that button. A button greys out when it would leave you with none. More filters holds Area, Frequency and Recognition.

3 · Seeing and undoing

Everything switched on appears in the Filtering by strip. Tap the × on one to remove just that filter, or Clear all to drop the lot.

Reset, beside More filters, does the same and stays greyed out until something is on. Neither touches the clubs you have starred.

4 · Four ways to look

Table — every club, one row each. Tap a heading to sort, tap again to reverse. Sorting by Next meeting shows who meets soonest.

Calendar — the meeting patterns turned into real dates. Tap a date for everything happening that day. Underneath runs the full list of upcoming meetings — the next 365 days by default, narrowable to today, 7, 14, 30 or 90.

Breakdown — totals, plus a grid of which nights are busiest. Starred — card 6.

5 · Tap any club, star the good ones

Wherever a club name appears, tapping it opens its card: when it meets, the venue with a map link, and every remaining meeting up to 30 June 2027. Each date carries a calendar icon that adds that one meeting to Google Calendar, time and venue filled in.

The star beside a club — in the table, in the list under the calendar, or on its card — adds it to the Starred tab. Tap the star again to drop it.

PH on a date means a Singapore public holiday. The pattern says the club meets; most won't. Row colour throughout shows the meeting language.

6 · Starred, and taking it with you

Starred lists every meeting of your starred clubs for the next 365 days, ignoring the filters, and marks any date where two of them fall together. Export just those meetings as a dated CSV.

Stars live in this browser, so they are still there when you come back — but not on your phone as well. Copy link makes a web address that carries them: open it anywhere, or send it to someone.

Up on the tab line, CSV downloads the filtered club list with more columns than the table shows, and Copy puts it on your clipboard for Excel or an email. Both follow your filters, so narrow things down first.

On a phone: in Safari or Chrome tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It gets its own icon, opens full screen without the address bar, and keeps working when the signal at the venue doesn't.

This is a personal reference tool, not an official publication. It was compiled by Ramanathan S for his own use and for informal sharing among Toastmasters members. It is not produced, reviewed, endorsed or approved by Toastmasters International or by District 80, and it is not an official record of either. Toastmasters International, District 80 and all related names, logos and marks are the property of Toastmasters International; they appear here only to identify the clubs being listed.

Accuracy is not guaranteed. Every detail was compiled from publicly available club listings on 29 July 2026 and is a snapshot of that date. Meeting days, times, venues and formats change. Some source entries are ambiguous, incomplete or already out of date. Membership figures are the 2026-27 dashboard's current numbers and recognition is the 2025-26 final result, neither of them live. Always confirm directly with a club before attending or relying on anything shown here. This tool is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind, and is used entirely at your own risk.

About the dates. No meeting date here was published by a club. Every one is calculated from a recurrence rule — "2nd & 4th Thursday" — so a date appearing in this tool means only that the pattern falls on that day. Dates landing on a Singapore public holiday are marked PH (holiday dates per the Ministry of Manpower), but a great many clubs also skip AGM weeks, school holidays, exam periods and their own one-off cancellations, and none of that is knowable from the sources. The Starred tab inherits the same assumption. Treat every future date as a prediction.

Spotted something wrong? Tell the club, and tell whoever maintains the District 80 club directory — correcting it at the source fixes it for everyone.

Tools

One utility for running and visiting Toastmasters meetings in Singapore — a club finder, a programme sheet, timing lights and an Ah-Counter's log. Tools open inside this app and keep running when you switch, so a live timer is not lost when you tap Ah-Counter. Nothing is uploaded.

Before the meeting

Planning and visiting

D80 Club Finder

For anyone looking for a club to visit

Every District 80 club — 217 of them, across 9 divisions and 45 areas — with when and where each one meets, turned into real dates you can actually turn up to.

  • Filter by division, language, day, time and format; Today and This week answer the usual question in one tap
  • A calendar that expands "2nd & 4th Thursday" into dates, and marks Singapore public holidays
  • Star the clubs you like — the Starred tab lists their meetings for the year ahead and flags any date where two fall together
  • Add a single meeting to Google Calendar, export a CSV, or share your starred list as a link

Programme Sheet Builder

For the Toastmaster of the Day

Fill in the panel on the left and the agenda redraws on the right. Built around Nee Soon East's sheet, but Club Setup is the first panel — put your own club's name, number, district line, venue and committee on it in about five minutes.

  • Pick Pathway → Level → Project and the detail line, duration and timing lights fill in from the official catalogue
  • Untick a role that isn't running today and it drops out of the roster and the agenda together
  • Add or remove a prepared speech and its paired evaluation moves with it
  • Drag segments to reorder, or apply your club's standard timings to retime the whole meeting at once
  • Save a meeting to a folder on your own computer and reopen it from the dropdown — handy for starting next month from last month's
  • Download a print-ready A4 PDF, a JPG under 500 KB for WhatsApp, or the sheet as an editable HTML page

In the room

Meeting roles, live

TMtimer

For the Timer

Timing lights on whatever screen you have. Pick a segment, name the speaker, press Start — the screen runs white, green, amber, red, with a bell thirty seconds after red.

  • Presets for Prepared Speech, Table Topics and Evaluation, or set the three times yourself
  • Reset logs the speaker — name, segment and time — and clears for the next one; switch segment or adjust a time mid-speech and the lights, bell and log all follow
  • Copy the whole list at the end for your Timer's report — the log is also saved in this browser, so a reload no longer wipes it
Open the timer →

The Ah-Counter's Log

For the Ah-Counter

Count crutch words without losing your place. One tap per crutch, one row per speaker, and a report at the end you can read out or hand over.

  • Add speakers as they're called; drag to reorder, and use letter keys once you're quick
  • Undo the last count with one tap — or Z — when you mis-tap
  • Add a remark against any line for the detail a number can't carry
  • Copy the report, or download it as a CSV

Please read

These tools are still being built. They are shared early because they are useful early, not because they are finished. Expect errors, missing cases and behaviour that changes between visits. Check anything that matters — a meeting time, a project duration, a speaker's count — before you rely on it in front of a room.

Not official. This is a personal project by one member. It is not produced, endorsed, reviewed or supported by Toastmasters International, by District 80, or by any club. Toastmasters International owns its names, marks and educational programme material; nothing here claims any connection to it. For anything official, go to toastmasters.org or toastmastersd80.org.

Club data is a snapshot. The club finder was compiled from published District 80 and Toastmasters International sources on 29 July 2026. It is not live. Venue changes, AGMs, public-holiday cancellations and one-off changes appear in none of it — confirm with the club before turning up.

Your data stays with you. Starred clubs, saved programme sheets, the timer log and counts are stored in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account and no tracking. Clearing your browser data clears them too.

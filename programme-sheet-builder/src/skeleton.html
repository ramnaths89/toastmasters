How the Programme Sheet Builder works

Fill in the panel on the left — the sheet on the right redraws as you type. Your work is kept in this browser automatically; the green ✓ at the top-left confirms it. Press 💾 Save to also keep it as a file you can reopen later. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

Saving your meetings

💾 Save
The first time, you pick a folder to keep meetings in — choose the folder the builder itself sits in. Name the meeting and it becomes a .json file there. From then on it saves itself as you work, so there is nothing to remember.
Saved meetings
The dropdown beside 💾 reopens any meeting in that folder. ＋ Save as a new file keeps the current one and starts another — handy for copying last month's agenda into this month's. If the browser asks to reconnect the folder after a restart, that is normal; pick it once and it remembers.

The rest of the toolbar

⚖ Balance
Nudges the two FLEXIBLE segments — Break and Table Topics — so the meeting finishes on time. The chip to its left shows where you currently finish.
⭳ Download
HTML reopens and stays editable anywhere. PDF saves a print-ready A4 file of about 450 KB straight away, with no dialog. JPG is an image of the sheet, under 450 KB — for WhatsApp and email.
Printing on paper
Download the PDF and print that, or press Ctrl + P on the downloaded HTML. There is no print button here any more — the browser dialog's destination setting quietly decided your file size, and the PDF above is the reliable answer.
↺ Reset
Back to the blank club template. This clears every name you have typed, so use it to start a fresh meeting — not to undo a mistake.

Filling in a meeting

Club Setup
Club name, district line, venue, committee and district officers. Rarely changes — refresh it after the AGM handover.
Meeting Info
Title, date, start and end times, and announcements. Each announcement goes on its own line.
Meeting Roles
Untick anything not running tonight and its agenda items switch off. Leave a role blank and the sheet prints a red TBD and lists it as still open.
Speeches & Evaluators
Pick Pathway → Level → Project and the timing fills in from the official catalog. Click ▾ on the Project box to browse all 64, or type to search. Drag the ⠿ grip to reorder speakers.
Programme Segments
Click any row to open it and change the title, holder or duration. ⏱ Apply club standard timings resets the durations to the club standard without touching names.

Two more things

Hover any row on the sheet itself and hit ✎ Edit to change it in place. Drag the divider between the panes to resize them, or double-click it for 50/50.

Timing lights follow the slot automatically — red lands a minute before the slot ends, and 🔔 is thirty seconds after that. Type your own numbers and they stay put; ↺ follow the slot again hands control back.

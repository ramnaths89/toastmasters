TOASTMASTERSTOOLS

Built by Ramanathan S · District 80 · Singapore

Small, single-page web tools for Toastmasters clubs and meetings. Everything here runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without a signal.

Tools

D80 Club Meeting Finder

Every District 80 club, when it meets, and where — filter by division, area, language, day, time and format. Star your own club and it hides anything that clashes, so you can see at a glance which clubs you could actually visit.

Put it on your phone Open a tool, then:
  1. iPhone — in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android — in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
It gets its own icon, opens full screen, and works offline afterwards. On iPhone this has to be done in Safari — Chrome on iOS cannot add to the home screen.

These tools are personal projects by an individual member. They are not official Toastmasters International or District 80 publications, are not endorsed by either, and the data in them may be out of date. Always confirm meeting details with the club before turning up.

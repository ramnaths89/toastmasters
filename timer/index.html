How to use
  1. Pick a segment — or set Green / Amber / Red with ± or by typing (m:ss).
  2. Enter the speaker's name (optional — blank auto-fills as "PS 1", "TT 2", …).
  3. Start — the screen and centre word go WHITE → GREEN → AMBER → RED, then BELL rings after the extra seconds set beside the bell.
  4. Reset logs the speaker (name · segment · time) and zeroes the clock. Pressed mid-run it rolls straight into the next speaker; Stop just pauses.
  5. Nothing locks while the clock runs. Started on the wrong segment? Switch it — or adjust any time — mid-speech and the lights, the bell and the log all follow.
  6. In the log: click a name or time to edit it, change the segment with its dropdown, ✕ removes a row. Copy exports the list.
  7. On a phone, tap Test once before the meeting starts. That unlocks audio and confirms the bell is loud enough for the room.

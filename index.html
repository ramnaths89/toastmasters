Toastmasters
Tools

Four instruments · one room

One utility for running and visiting Toastmasters meetings in Singapore — a club finder, a programme sheet that covers chapter meetings and speech contests, timing lights, and an Ah-Counter's log. Tools open inside this app and keep running when you switch, so a live timer is not lost when you tap Ah-Counter. Nothing is uploaded.

Work in progress — expect rough edges

Before the meeting

01 — 02 · Planning

01 Finder

D80 Club Finder

For anyone looking for a club to visit

Every District 80 club — 217 of them, across 9 divisions and 45 areas — with when and where each one meets, turned into real dates you can actually turn up to.

  • Filter by division, language, day, time and format; Today and This week in one tap
  • A calendar that expands “2nd & 4th Thursday” into dates, and marks Singapore public holidays
  • Star clubs — the Starred tab lists their year ahead and flags clash days
  • Add a meeting to Google Calendar, export a CSV, or share starred clubs as a link

02 Sheet

Programme Sheet

For the Toastmaster of the Day

Fill the left panel; the agenda redraws on the right. Built around Nee Soon East, but Club Setup is the first panel — five minutes to put your club on it. Chapter meeting or speech contest — switching replaces the running order, not the officers.

  • Pathway → Level → Project fills duration and timing lights from the official catalogue
  • Contest mode: contestants, contest committee, 165-minute template to 21:30
  • Break flexes 10–20 minutes (nominal 15) so a blank chapter sheet still totals 150
  • Save a meeting as JSON on your own disk; download A4 PDF, JPG under 500 KB, or HTML
Open the builder →

Running a different club? Customise it for yours → — Club Setup, contest vs chapter, saving, and how to make your club the permanent default. Officer names on the public sheet are placeholders, not real people.

In the room

03 — 04 · Live

03 Timer

TMtimer

For the Timer

Timing lights on whatever screen you have. Pick a segment, name the speaker, press Start — the screen runs white, green, amber, red, with a bell thirty seconds after red.

  • Presets for Prepared Speech, Table Topics and Evaluation, or set the three times yourself
  • Reset logs the speaker and clears for the next; switch segment mid-speech and the lights follow
  • Copy the list for the Timer's report — the log is saved in this browser
Open the timer →
04 Log

Ah-Counter's Log

For the Ah-Counter

Count crutch words without losing your place. One tap per crutch, one row per speaker, and a report at the end you can read out or hand over.

  • Add speakers as they're called; drag to reorder; letter keys once you're quick
  • Undo the last count with one tap — or Z — when you mis-tap
  • Remarks on any line; copy the report or download CSV
Open the log →

Please read

These tools are still being built. They are shared early because they are useful early, not because they are finished. Expect errors, missing cases and behaviour that changes between visits. Check anything that matters — a meeting time, a project duration, a speaker's count — before you rely on it in front of a room.

Not official. This is a personal project by one member. It is not produced, endorsed, reviewed or supported by Toastmasters International, by District 80, or by any club. Toastmasters International owns its names, marks and educational programme material; nothing here claims any connection to it. For anything official, go to toastmasters.org or toastmastersd80.org.

Club data is a snapshot. The club finder was compiled from published District 80 and Toastmasters International sources on 29 July 2026. It is not live. Venue changes, AGMs, public-holiday cancellations and one-off changes appear in none of it — confirm with the club before turning up.

Your data stays with you. Starred clubs, saved programme sheets, the timer log and counts are stored in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account and no tracking. Clearing your browser data clears them too.

On a club laptop. Chrome or Edge can Install this page as a window. For a double-clickable desktop wrap that does not use file://, see desktop/ — python3 desktop/launch.py opens an app window on a local origin.
